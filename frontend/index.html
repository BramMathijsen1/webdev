Walk into the films

Pick a story, then follow it from one filming location to the next.

Why these places

Films and series turn real streets into stories. LMML brings together the places in Paris and London where four of them were shot: Amélie, Emily in Paris, Love Actually and Sherlock. Stand where the camera stood, and you see a city you thought you knew with new eyes. Every place is described from documented facts, each with its source.

How to read a story

  • Each story follows one film or series, chapter by chapter, from stop to stop. You can go back and forth as you like.
  • A stop tells you how to get there, how to recognise the place, what happened there on screen and where the camera stood.
  • The text adapts to you: longer or shorter, simpler or more difficult, with games for young visitors or references for scholars.
  • Each story opens in its own theme; the theme menu at the top changes it.
  • On site, the QR code of a place opens it as a stop of the story you are following.
